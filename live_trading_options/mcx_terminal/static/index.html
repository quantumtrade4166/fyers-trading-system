Settings

Order placement

Kotak takes no MARKET orders through the API, so every order is a LIMIT. Choose where an "at the market" limit comes from. A BUY goes at reference + buffer, a SELL at reference − buffer, rounded to the tick.

Straddle / Strangle strike gap

Show only every Nth strike in the Straddle and Strangle tables.

Replays

Every day you place an order is recorded automatically (from 15 min before your first order to the close). Download a day and double-click the file: it opens this terminal offline and plays the day back — prices, your trades, positions, P&L, risk guard and graphs exactly as they were. Files are also backed up to Google Drive.
