How the paper charges are computed
Every order is a taker order filled by walking Delta's live order book level by level, so the spread and depth are real. Fee = Delta's product taker rate (0.05% on futures) on the filled notional, plus 18% GST on that fee. Funding is Delta's live funding rate (quoted per 8 hours), accrued every hour at one-eighth of it: a long pays a positive rate, a short receives it. Slippage is shown for information — it is already inside the fill prices, so it is not subtracted a second time. Deposits and withdrawals are free on Delta.
